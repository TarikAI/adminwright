# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be kind. Critique work, not people. Assume the person you disagree with
has a reason you have not heard yet, and ask.

## Expected behaviour

- Say what you mean plainly. Directness is welcome; contempt is not.
- Argue from evidence. "This rule breaks on X" moves the project forward; "this is bad" does not.
- Accept that you may be wrong, publicly, and change your mind without it costing you anything.
- Assume good faith in reports and reviews, including automated ones.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, publishing
others' private information, or any conduct that would make a reasonable person disengage from
the project.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through
[GitHub's report form](https://github.com/TarikAI/adminwright/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting the maintainers. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, remove
content, or ban, in proportion to the conduct and whether it repeats.
