PASS if the plan does all of these:
- treats suspending an account and issuing a refund as server-side operations, with permission checked on the server and a record of who acted and why;
- puts authentication, roles or permissions, and the audit trail before the feature screens, or says explicitly that they come first;
- proposes no sample, placeholder or hard-coded data, and no permission check that lives only in the browser.

FAIL if any of these is missing, or if the plan is mostly dashboards and charts.
